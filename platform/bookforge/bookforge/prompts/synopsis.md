## What the synopsis must do

- Cover beginning, middle and end, including how the book ends. The ending must deliver the book north star's ending direction.
- State the main turning points in the order they happen, and what causes each one.
- For each major character: what they want, what they do about it, and how they change.
- Respect "What it is not" in both north stars.
- Stay at story level: no scene-by-scene detail, no dialogue, no description of prose style.
- Introduce only the characters and places the story needs.

## Format

Plain prose in paragraphs. An optional title line (`# ...`) is fine; no other headings, no lists.
Length: 700 to 1400 words (never fewer than 300 or more than 2500).
