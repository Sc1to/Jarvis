A check found passages of a story plan that share many words. Decide whether they describe the SAME event told again, or different events that only happen to share words, people or a place.

{passages}

## Decide

- Same event: the same thing happens (or is retold) in both: the same discovery, arrival, escape, decision, confrontation or action carried out again as if it had not happened before.
- Not the same event: a later passage that builds on the earlier one (a consequence, a reaction, a payoff of something planted before), a deliberate echo, a routine that recurs by nature, or different actions in the same place.

Return exactly this JSON and nothing else:

{"same_event": true, "reason": "one sentence"}
