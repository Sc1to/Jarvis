## Format

Start with a title line: `# <working title of the series>`

Then exactly these sections, in this order, each as a level-2 heading:

## Premise
Two to four sentences: who the story is about, what they want, what stands in the way.

## Themes
Two to four themes, each a short phrase with one sentence on how the story explores it.

## Emotional promise
What the reader should feel while reading and when they finish. One short paragraph.

## Tone
Voice, mood and register, with the world's texture (genre, era, setting). One short paragraph.

## Ending direction
Where the series as a whole is heading and what kind of ending it earns. Do not give plot details, give direction.

## What it is not
Four to six things this series must not become (clichés, genres, tones, plot moves to avoid).

The whole document must fit on one page: about 350 to 450 words, never more than 600.
