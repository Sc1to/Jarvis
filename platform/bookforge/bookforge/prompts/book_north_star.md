## Format

Start with a title line: `# <title of this book>`

Then exactly these sections, in this order, each as a level-2 heading:

## Premise
Two to four sentences: what happens to whom in this book, and what is at stake.

## Themes
Two to four themes for this book, each a short phrase with one sentence on how this book explores it. They may be a subset or sharpening of the series themes.

## Emotional promise
What the reader feels over the course of this book and at its end. One short paragraph.

## Tone
Voice and mood of this book, and how it differs from or deepens the series tone. One short paragraph.

## Realism
How faithful to the real world this book must be. If it is the same as the series north star, write "As the series." and nothing else, or add one sentence of exceptions after it. If this book differs (a flashback book in a real period of a space opera, a book with a stated exception to the physics), start with a short label and say how, in one or two sentences. The book's statement wins over the series' where they differ.

## Ending direction
How this book ends: the change in the protagonist, what is resolved, and what stays open for later books (if any). Direction, not a scene.

## What it is not
Four to six things this book must not do, including any that follow from the series north star.

Fit on one page: about 350 to 450 words, never more than 600.
