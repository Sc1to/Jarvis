# Synopsis (TEST DATA: several viewpoints)

In the mountain city of Karth, the river guild closes its gates against the grain merchants, and the quarrel begins at the Harvest Fair.

Dara Venn, a guild apprentice, discovers that the guildmaster has been selling river rights to a foreign house.

Tomas Oakby, a grain merchant, is arrested at the gates and learns that the guild's ledgers have been altered.

Dara steals the true ledger from the guild hall and flees the city by the river road.

Tomas escapes the city jail with the help of a sympathetic warden and joins Dara on the road.

Together they reach the foreign house's river fort and expose the sale before the assembled delegates.

The delegates strip the guildmaster of his seal and reopen the gates, and Dara and Tomas return to Karth as witnesses.

The new council grants the apprentices a charter and Dara takes the first seat for the guild's youngest members.
