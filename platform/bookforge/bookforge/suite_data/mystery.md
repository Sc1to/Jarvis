# Synopsis (TEST DATA: a mystery with setups and reveals)

Inspector Hale arrives at Wren House to investigate the death of the heiress Lydia Ashby, which the family insists was an accident.

In the library Hale notices a clock stopped at ten past nine and a locked drawer in the heiress's desk.

The butler Pryce lies about where he was that night, and Hale learns the cook saw him on the stairs at nine.

Hale opens the locked drawer and finds a half-finished will that disinherits the nephew Edmund.

Edmund confronts Hale and claims the will is a forgery, and the stopped clock is explained as a servant's mistake.

Hale discovers that the clock was stopped on purpose to fix the time of death at ten past nine.

Pryce confesses that he wound the clock back for Edmund and that Lydia was already dead at eight.

Hale proves that Edmund poisoned the heiress's evening tea, and Edmund is arrested in the drawing room.
