# Synopsis (TEST DATA: five planted problems)

Ines Vale, a courier, delivers a sealed case to the harbor office. The clerk Dorrin Pell opens the case and burns its papers in the stove.

Ines later presents the papers to the magistrate, who reads them aloud in open court. Harbor Master Corvane releases Ines, but only on the condition that she hands over the case, and she has already handed it over.

Dorrin gives Ines the vault key without a word of explanation, and Ines opens the vault with a brass key she was never given.

The magistrate dismisses the charges against the Marsh family.
