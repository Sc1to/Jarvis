# Synopsis (TEST DATA: book two of a series; the state at the start is inherited from book one)

Captain Rhee returns to Port Lantern with the damaged ship Marigold, still bound by the debt she signed at the end of the first book.

She learns that the harbor board has seized the Marigold's cargo because of her old debt.

Rhee negotiates with the harbor board and agrees to carry a sealed crate to the northern docks in exchange for the cargo.

During the voyage north she discovers that the crate holds the board's records of the debt.

Rhee must choose between delivering the crate and using its records to cancel her own debt, and she opens it.

She finds that the debt was never lawful and brings the records to the harbor court.

The court voids the debt and Rhee sails the Marigold out of Port Lantern as a free captain.
