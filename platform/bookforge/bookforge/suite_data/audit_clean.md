Ines Vale, a courier, delivers a sealed case to the harbor office, where the clerk Dorrin Pell takes it into the vault.

Dorrin opens the case that night and finds ledgers that show the harbor master Teague has been selling cargo twice.

Ines is arrested for the theft of the ledgers when Teague accuses her, and Dorrin visits her in the cell to ask what she knows.

At the hearing Dorrin testifies that he opened the case himself, and the magistrate frees Ines and orders Teague arrested.
